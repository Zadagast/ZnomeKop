# Third-party tilesets (CC0 / free)

ZnomeKop uses these asset packs for all in-game art. There is no procedural art fallback.

## Stealthix — 1-Bit RPG Tileset 32×32
- Source: https://stealthix.itch.io/1-bit-rpg-tileset
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0)
- Used for: overworld terrain tiles, tall props

## Kenney — 1-Bit Pack
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/1-bit-pack
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0) — see `kenney/License.txt`
- Used for: player sprite frames (16×16 scaled to 32×32), occasional accents

## Hexany Ives — Monster Menagerie
- Source: https://hexany-ives.itch.io/hexanys-monster-menagerie
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0)
- Used for: creature icons (`creatures-table-32-32.png`)
